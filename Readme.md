EV Charging Infrastructure Analysis

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing EV charging infrastructure across the United States using the U.S. Department of Energy Alternative Fuel Stations dataset.

Project Overview

The rapid adoption of Electric Vehicles (EVs) has increased the demand for reliable charging infrastructure. This project analyzes the distribution of EV charging stations across the United States, highlighting infrastructure growth, charging networks, accessibility, and geographic coverage.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to identify infrastructure gaps, monitor network expansion, and compare charging availability across states and providers.

Business Problem

As EV adoption accelerates, governments, charging providers, and policymakers require accurate insights into charging infrastructure to support investment decisions.

This dashboard helps answer:

Where are charging stations concentrated?
Which charging networks dominate?
How rapidly is infrastructure expanding?
What proportion of stations are publicly accessible?
Which states require additional infrastructure?

Key Insights
California has the highest number of EV charging stations.
Public charging stations account for approximately 93.6% of all stations.
ChargePoint Network has the largest charging network.
EV infrastructure expansion accelerated significantly after 2020.

These observations are based on the dashboard and underlying dataset.
