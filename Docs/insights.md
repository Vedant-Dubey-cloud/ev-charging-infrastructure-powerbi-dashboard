# Key Insights

## Executive Summary

The dashboard provides an overview of EV charging infrastructure across the United States, highlighting charging station availability, infrastructure growth, network providers, and accessibility.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. California Leads EV Infrastructure

- California has the highest number of EV charging stations among all states.
- The state significantly outpaces other regions, reflecting its advanced EV adoption and charging infrastructure.

---

### 2. Public Charging Dominates

- Approximately 93.6% of charging stations are publicly accessible.
- Public infrastructure forms the majority of the national charging network, improving accessibility for EV users.

---

### 3. ChargePoint Network Has the Largest Presence

- ChargePoint operates the largest number of charging stations among all network providers.
- Other major providers contribute smaller shares of the overall infrastructure.

---

### 4. Rapid Infrastructure Expansion

- The number of charging stations has increased steadily over time.
- Infrastructure growth accelerates significantly after 2020, indicating increased investment in EV charging facilities.

---

### 5. Strong Level 2 Charging Availability

- Level 2 chargers represent the largest share of installed charging equipment.
- DC Fast Chargers are also widely available, supporting long-distance travel and faster charging.

---

### 6. High Operational Availability

- Most charging stations are currently operational.
- The relatively small number of inactive stations indicates a mature and well-maintained charging network.
