# Business Recommendations

## 1. Expand Infrastructure in Low-Coverage States

States with relatively fewer charging stations should be prioritized for future infrastructure investments to improve nationwide accessibility.

---

## 2. Continue Public Charging Expansion

Since public charging stations represent the majority of the network, continued investment in publicly accessible infrastructure can further support EV adoption.

---

## 3. Increase DC Fast Charger Deployment

Expanding DC Fast Charging stations along highways, urban corridors, and high-traffic locations can improve charging convenience for long-distance travel.

---

## 4. Strengthen Charging Network Coverage

Charging providers can evaluate underserved regions and expand their networks to improve geographic coverage and reduce charging gaps.

---

## 5. Support Data-Driven Infrastructure Planning

Government agencies and utility companies should use charging infrastructure analytics to identify regions requiring additional investment based on existing station distribution.

---

## 6. Monitor Infrastructure Growth

Regular monitoring of charging station deployment can help stakeholders evaluate progress toward transportation electrification goals and future infrastructure requirements.
